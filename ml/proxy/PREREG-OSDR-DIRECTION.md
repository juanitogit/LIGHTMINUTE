Before inspecting new numerical results, extract and freeze the published comparison rows/signs, figure citation, unit and visit mapping from the article or publisher source data. For each comparable visit and analyte compute the sign of the arithmetic mean of paired post-minus-personal-preflight-mean differences in native units, report counts of individual increase/decrease/tie, band flags in either direction and denominator with complete pairs. No p-values, population, causal or clinical claims. Declare count of agreements/disagreements/unevaluable and report every row. A successful comparison requires a documented matched analyte and visit even when all signs disagree.
