# Software hazard analysis · LIGHTMINUTE

**Scope.** These are consequences if someone relied on this simulator; no crew harm was measured. All injections used synthetic data. Severity expresses the possible consequence, independent of likelihood. Low means inconvenience, medium means added work or confusion recoverable by checking the record, high means a materially misleading or delayed decision, and critical means a potentially suppressed urgent escalation or disclosure of withheld sensitive information.

Each FI identifier links the mode to its named executable case and observed output in [the run table](fault-injection-results.md). MITIGATED-TESTED means the specified assertion passed at that interface. OPEN means the guard failed, raised an exception, or was absent. MITIGATED-UNTESTED would mean a documented control exists but was not exercised. Column “check / control” gives the detection method followed by the design behavior; the Evidence column names the executable case in `tests/fault_injection/run.py` (same FI identifier) or `browser.mjs` for the UI cases.

## Unsafe control actions (STPA-inspired)

| Control action | Unsafe context or timing | Constraint and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Issue a crew-facing card | Headline cites unsupported HR; latest HR is absent | Every measurement shown needs a source, unit and acquisition time. FI-02 now fact-checks displayed numbers; FI-10 states the age of a missing reading. |
| Withhold escalation or card | Generated urgency drops, provider initialization fails, or adaptation absorbs a sustained shift | Preserve engine urgency and fallback availability; distinguish changed physiology from a governed decision to relearn. FI-03 and FI-07 now pass their assertions; FI-13 passes its injection but regresses real-public calibration. |
| Send a report to ground | Unstructured content carries a withheld category | Consent must apply to the transmitted representation. FI-19 and FI-20 show the boundary. |
| Repeat an action on receipt | Delayed and retried messages arrive after the original | Use stable identities across retries and timestamps visible to the operator. FI-15 and FI-16 cover different guarantees. |
| Advise waiting for a ground reply | Card context represents a different mission than its scenario | Derive delay context from the same scenario used for routing. FI-17, FI-22 and FI-23 pass. |
| Continue displaying a case during outage | Fallback belongs to a different scenario or archived inputs lack freshness | Preserve actual case identity, retrieval time and source through the UI. FI-24 through FI-27. |

## Evidence and remaining exposure

Run from the repository root. `.venv/Scripts/python.exe tests/fault_injection/run.py` produced [27 observations](fault-injection-results.json), [console output](fault-run.log) and [per-case verdicts](fault-injection-results.md) at revision `05533ea`: **27/27 safe-behavior assertions passed** and the harness exited zero. The independent command `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/run_tests.py` recorded [111/111 passing](baseline-tests.log).

These results concern demo software. Hardware faults, network partitions and recovery after a lost client acknowledgement, whole-history audit replacement, clinical validity, protection from arbitrary generated text, and safe use in an actual mission remain unverified. The baseline guard changed engine behavior, so both preregistered studies were rerun on this revision and are reported in [autonomy v2](../autonomy/RESULTS.md) and [public data v2](../../ml/proxy/RESULTS.md). The autonomy v2 numbers are current; the MMASH calibration gate fails on this revision (66.37% against a 90–98% tolerance), so FI-13 is a shipping blocker even though its injection passes.

The evidence was collected at revision `05533ea`; unrelated untracked preregistration files for the optional core-temperature study were present but are not imported by the engine. [SHA-256 manifest](checkout-sha256.txt) fingerprints the Python engine sources and built JS/HTML observed after the run.
