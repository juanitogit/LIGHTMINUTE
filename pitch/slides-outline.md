# Slides outline (6 slides max)

One idea per slide, one number per slide, no paragraph text.

## 1 — The problem
Title: *Mars is 20 light-minutes away.*
- The crew doctor is the patient.
- With four people, the population average does not exist.
- A dashboard is not a decision.

## 2 — What TETHER does
Title: *Your body's baseline, even when Earth is out of reach.*
- Learn the person (N-of-1), not the population.
- Read coordinated shifts, not single spikes.
- Output a cited Decision Card: what changed / why / do now / escalate if / what to tell Earth.
- Works offline, and with the LLM unplugged.

## 3 — The demo moment
Title: *Normal for the population. Unusual for him.*
- Screenshot: three lenses at day 16, HR 72.6 bpm, `hidden_in_range`.
- Screenshot: syndrome score crossing 2.33 while no single indicator does.
- One line: "a fixed range stays silent exactly when it matters."

## 4 — Validation, with the losing column visible
Title: *70% vs 18% — and where we lose.*
- Table: 18/38/91 vs 70/96/100 (mild/moderate/strong), equal false-alarm budget.
- Heterogeneous crew: population 27% strong vs personal 88–100%.
- Footnote: single-indicator N-of-1 is not better than a population rule in homogeneous crews.

## 5 — Honesty as a feature
Title: *Our own model was wrong. We published it.*
- Real wearable data: 95% interval covered 21.6% of minutes.
- Cause found (forgetting factor), fix verified: 93.4%.
- Not a medical device · synthetic only · every limitation in the model card.

- Built: engine, validation, data layer, demo. Next: app shell, protocol runner, ground console.
